Frequently Asked Questions

How do I translate a conversation?

Open Polyverba, choose the languages you want to use, and start a voice translation session. Allow microphone access when prompted.

Why isn't voice translation working?

Check your internet connection and microphone permissions. Make sure the selected languages are supported. Close and reopen the app if the issue continues.

Why isn't camera translation working?

Verify that camera access is enabled in your device settings. Use adequate lighting and keep text clearly visible.

What if translations are inaccurate?

Speak clearly, reduce background noise, and verify the selected languages. Automated translations can contain errors, especially with names, slang, or technical terminology.

Subscriptions and Billing

Polyverba offers optional subscription plans for enhanced translation features. Available plans, pricing, and billing terms are shown in the app before purchase.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Polyverba, navigate to Plans & Subscription, and select Restore purchases. Use the same app store account associated with the original purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription on iPhone or iPad?

Open Settings, select your Apple Account, tap Subscriptions, choose Polyverba, and follow the cancellation instructions.

Apple subscription help: support.apple.com/billing

How do I cancel a subscription on Android?

Open Google Play, go to Payments & subscriptions, select Subscriptions, choose Polyverba, and follow the cancellation instructions.

Google Play subscription help: Google Play Help

Why are subscription plans unavailable?

Check your connection, confirm your app store account is active, and try again later. If plans remain unavailable, contact support with your device type and app version.

Subscription purchases, cancellations, and refunds are generally managed by the app store through which you subscribed.
