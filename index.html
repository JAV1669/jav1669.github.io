2. Information PolyVerba May Collect

The information processed by PolyVerba depends on which features you choose to use.

Account Information

If you create an account, PolyVerba may process:

  • Email address
  • User or account identifier
  • Authentication and account-management information

This information is used to authenticate users, provide account features, synchronize supported information, maintain account security, prevent abuse, and manage the Service.

Translation and User-Generated Content

When you use translation features, PolyVerba may process text or other content that you provide or generate through the Service. Depending on your settings and the feature being used, translation or meeting history may be stored locally on your device or synchronized with PolyVerba's cloud services.

You can control supported history features through the application.

Voice and Audio

PolyVerba requests microphone access when you use spoken-language features. Audio may be transmitted to speech-processing or translation services when necessary to recognize speech and provide the requested translation.

Microphone access is not required when you are not using a feature that needs audio input.

Camera and Photos

PolyVerba may request camera or photo access when you use features such as Live Lens, sign translation, menu translation, or other camera-assisted translation features.

Images or image content may be processed as necessary to recognize and translate visible text. PolyVerba does not require camera access for ordinary text or voice translation features that do not use the camera.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version of PolyVerba may display advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Paid PolyVerba subscription tiers may be provided without advertising.

When advertising is enabled and an ad request is permitted, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's approximate or general location;
  • App and product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and advertising interactions;

This information may be used for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes.

Google states that information automatically collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

Advertising identifiers may also be subject to controls provided by your device operating system.

For additional information about Google's privacy practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy.
